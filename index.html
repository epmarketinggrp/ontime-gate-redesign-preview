Gate Questions, Answered Straight

How much does electric gate repair cost in Miami?

It depends on what failed. A sensor adjustment or a remote issue costs far less than a new motor or control board. That's why we offer a free onsite estimate: we look at the gate, tell you the exact price, and you decide.

Do you charge to come out and look at my gate?

No. The onsite estimate is free. You pay only if you approve the work.

How fast can you get here?

Usually the same day you call. We're available 24/7, so you can call and book any time. Our technicians work Sunday to Thursday 6 AM to 10 PM and Friday 6 AM to 1 PM.

My gate is stuck and my car is trapped. What do I do?

Most electric gates have a manual release that lets you open them by hand, and we can walk you through it over the phone. Don't force the gate, since that can damage the motor or arm. Call (786) 946-5181 and we'll come fix the cause the same day.

Is the work guaranteed?

Yes. Every repair and installation comes with a warranty. If something we worked on fails, call us and we'll come back and make it right.

Should I repair my gate opener or replace it?

Repair it if the problem is one part and the rest of the unit is in good shape. If the opener is old, has failed several times, or parts are hard to get, replacing it usually costs less over time. We'll give you both prices so you can choose.
